   53  ### Landed beyond the original M1 list
   54  
   55  First-use feedback moved several things forward:
   56  
   57  - **No F-keys.** Many keyboards lack them and the browser claims several, so the bar
   58    shows ⌘-chords and is clickable. Chords a tab can never own (⌘W/⌘N/⌘T) hang off a
   59    ⌘K leader; Tauri adds the direct forms later without relearning.
   60  - **Pick modal** (`Pick.tsx`) — the v2 info-tier filterable list, built as the reusable
   61    one M2's choosers and M3's `+` picker extend. Drives ⌘P, ⌘⇧P, ⌘K, ⌘O, ⌘, and ⌘/.
   62  - **Touched-files list** derived from `session.jsonl`, per-session and reload-proof.
   63  - **Slash commands** — `/clear`, `/new`, `/model`, `/files`, `/touched`, `/help` work;
   64    `/compact` and `/rewind` name the milestone they arrive in rather than failing silently.
   65  - **`@` completes** repo files inline and produces a chip, not text.
   66  - **`show_files` replaced `open_in_panel`** — the brain names paths, the app decides how
   67    to display each. Auto-open-on-write was built and then removed: inferring intent from
   68    a write only covers "here is what I changed".
   69  - **`fs.tree`, `folders.list`, `fs.wrote`, `files.shown`** added to §3.
   70  
   71  ### Known gaps carried forward
   72  
   73  - `⌘O` lists folders but cannot switch repo — one repo per window needs a backend per

   76  - `~/.aicommander/config.json` supports `apiKey` and `apiKeyEnv`, but nothing writes it
   77    from the UI yet.
